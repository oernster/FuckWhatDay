Know what day it is.
Eventually.

FuckWhatDay is the industry's first enterprise grade Gregorian weekday resolution platform written entirely in Brainfuck. You give it a date. It gives you the day. Between those two events it executes several hundred thousand instructions, none of which were strictly necessary.

No part of the calculation is delegated to a sensible programming language.

Try it in your browser Read the source, if you dare

FuckWhatDay exists because WhatDay already solved this problem sensibly.

Trusted by absolutely nobody, including:

  • The Department of Redundancy Department
  • Calendars Anonymous
  • The Year 1900 (not a leap year)
  • Big Tuesday
  • Your Wristwatch, reluctantly
